# GradePredict AI: User Manual

## Signing up and signing in

1. Open the site and choose Register. Pick your role. Students also choose a program and year level.
2. After registering you will see a message that the account is waiting for approval. An administrator, or a teacher (for student and parent sign-ups), approves it. Teacher sign-ups need an administrator.
3. Once approved, sign in with your email and password. Accounts created by an administrator use a temporary password and ask you to choose your own the first time.
4. Forgot your password? Use "Forgot Password" on the sign-in page.

## Student

Dashboard: your overall predicted grade, status, trend, and each subject at a glance. Use the Table or Progress switch to change the view.
My Subjects: add subjects prescribed for your program, year and semester. You cannot add subjects while a balance is unpaid.
Data Inputs: update attendance, assignments, quizzes and participation. Past grades are set by your teacher.
Predictions: the predicted grade for each subject, how it was computed, trend and confidence. Use Flag for Review if a number looks wrong.
Grade History: every change to your scores.
My Finance: fees, payments and balance.
Profile and Settings: both pages show the same screen. Change your photo (tap the camera), edit your name and email (Edit profile), turn notification switches on or off, change your password, and pick Light or Dark theme. Program and year level are set by the school office.

## Teacher

Dashboard and My Students show only the classes assigned to you. Open a student to update scores or past grades. Add Student creates a new student account or enrolls an existing one in your class. Approvals lists sign-ups waiting for a decision.

## Parent

Dashboard lists the children linked to your account. Open one to see predicted grades and status. Linking is done at registration (student number or email) or by an administrator.

## Administrator

Programs and Majors: add a program, set its length (from 6 months to 4 years) and edit its code, name or description at any time. Year level choices follow the program length, so a 6-month course only offers Year 1.
Account generator: Many accounts or One account. Paste names (one per line, "Juan Dela Cruz" or "Dela Cruz, Juan"), choose role, email style and domain, press Preview, check the list, then Create. Passwords appear once on the results page. Download the CSV or print it and hand each person their details.
Users: change roles, delete, allow someone to approve sign-ups, create a single user.
Approvals: approve or reject sign-ups.
Grading Sheets (Registrar): pick the school year, then filter by program, year level, semester and subject. The Consolidated tab shows every subject with each student's GWA, the Per subject tab shows the grading sheet for one subject, and the Honors outlook tab ranks students by GWA and marks each Qualified or Not qualified with the reasons. Download Excel workbook gives the consolidated sheet, the honors sheet and a sheet for every subject. Download CSV gives the tab you are on. Print makes a landscape sheet with signature lines. On the Honors outlook tab, set the honor limits to match the student handbook.
Model Accuracy: after a subject ends, record final grades. The page compares them with the last prediction and shows average error, how many were within 5 points, grade-band agreement and how well at-risk students were caught.
Subjects: set custom prediction weights for a subject (must total 100%).
Settings: school settings, backup and restore.
Reports and Activity Logs: statistics, CSV export, printable report, and a log of who did what.

## Dark mode

Dark mode applies to the signed-in screens only. The sign-in, registration and public pages are always light. Switch it from Settings > Theme.
